# Task: define a testable visual and interaction target

## Inputs

- Game fantasy, camera, genre, platforms: [CONTEXT]
- Representative gameplay states: [STATES]
- Approved references with captures: [REFERENCES]
- Performance/accessibility budgets: [BUDGETS]

## Assignment

Choose references comparable in camera, platform, interaction density, and art
direction. Analyze complete frames, not isolated assets or promotional art.
Define observable criteria for composition, silhouettes, scale, materials,
lighting, motion, effects, UI, state readability, accessibility, and technical
stability. Separate artistic preference from functional readability and measured
performance.

## Deliverables

- Weighted rubric with examples of pass/fail evidence.
- Same-state capture routes for quiet, dense, menu, success, failure, and motion.
- Target-device image-quality and frame/memory budgets.
- A gap assessment that labels unknown or uninspected states.
- Art-direction decisions that preserve originality and asset provenance.

## Gate

Do not assign “AAA” or a numeric score from one beauty shot. A score is valid only
for the captured build, states, rubric version, reviewers, and tested hardware.
