# Task: teach the real mechanic through play

## Inputs

- Validated slice and target players: [PATH/PLAYERS]
- Supported inputs/platforms/locales: [TARGETS]
- Observed first-session failures: [EVIDENCE]

## Assignment

Observe newcomers before designing instruction. Separate control confusion,
perception/feedback failure, terminology, and actual mechanic difficulty. Build
the smallest contextual sequence that gives the player a safe action, immediate
feedback, an early win, then independence. Prefer the world and mechanic over a
manual or long modal. Support skip/replay without corrupting progression.

## Deliverables

- First-session beat map with learning objective and exit condition per beat.
- Input-equivalent, accessible, localized implementation.
- Recovery for ignored prompts, repeated failure, interruption, and return visits.
- Event/observation plan limited to the onboarding decisions being made.
- Fresh-player test results and comparison with the pre-change baseline.

## Gate

Do not call onboarding successful because players clicked through it. Verify they
perform and explain the mechanic after prompts are removed.
