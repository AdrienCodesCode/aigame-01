# Task: turn playtest evidence into the next decision

## Inputs

- Build/version, sample, recruitment, and test script: [CONTEXT]
- Events/funnels/performance data: [DATA]
- Observations/interviews/surveys/bugs: [QUALITATIVE DATA]
- Original hypothesis and stop criteria: [QUESTION]

## Assignment

Validate data quality before interpreting outcomes. Reconstruct the intended loop
and identify where players perceived, decided, acted, recovered, succeeded, or
left. Separate technical failure, discoverability, control skill, motivation, and
core-design problems. Compare what players said with observable behavior without
treating either as infallible.

Quantify denominators and uncertainty. Segment only where sample size supports it.
List competing explanations and the cheapest discriminating experiment.

## Deliverables

- Data-quality warnings and five evidence-backed findings at most.
- Hypothesis outcome: supported, contradicted, or inconclusive.
- Ranked bugs versus design/communication issues.
- No more than three next changes, each with expected effect and success measure.
- Keep/change/pivot/stop recommendation and missing instrumentation.

## Gate

Do not produce a generic top-20 backlog from a small sample. Prefer one learning
cycle over many simultaneous changes that cannot be attributed.
